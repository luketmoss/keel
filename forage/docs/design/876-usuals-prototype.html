Usuals: meals you eat on repeat

A prototype for logging the same-ish meal fast. Oatmeal is oats and a nut butter almost every day, plus whatever fruit is around, in amounts that drift. A recipe is the wrong shape for that: it's fixed, and logging it hides the parts. This prototype tries four angles that share one idea: a meal in the log stays a set of foods you can edit, and anything reusable just gives you a filled-in starting point.

The working name for a reusable meal is a usual ("add my usual oatmeal"). It's a placeholder; template, base and go-to all work. The phone on the right is live: tap through it.

Angle 1: copy a meal, both directions

You asked for "copy breakfast to another day". You log when you eat, so the common case is probably the reverse: you're on today and want yesterday's breakfast. The prototype offers both.

Try it
  1. Today's Breakfast is empty. Tap Same as yesterday. All four foods land, grouped as Oatmeal, amounts exact.
  2. Tap ⋯ on Lunch, then Copy to…. Pick a day and a meal; the toast has Undo.

How it behaves

  • Pull: an empty meal suggests the same meal from the most recent day that has one, "Same as yesterday" or "Same as Thursday". One tap, no sheet.
  • Push: the meal's ⋯ menu has Copy to…: a day (Tomorrow, the next few days, or a date) and a meal slot, defaulting to the same slot.
  • Copied entries are new FoodLog rows with today's date and the same food, amount and unit. Their numbers are recomputed from the food, so a food you corrected since gives the corrected figures.
  • Copying into a meal that already has entries adds to it; nothing is replaced. Undo removes exactly what was copied.
  • Groups survive a copy: yesterday's Oatmeal arrives as Oatmeal.

Open question

Should "Same as…" look back further than the last day with that meal, say a short list of the last three distinct breakfasts? I kept it to one so it's a single tap. Detected combinations in the Add sheet (angle 2) cover the rest.

Angle 2: a usual adds its foods, not a recipe

Adding a usual writes one entry per food, grouped under the usual's name. From then on they're ordinary entries: change an amount, remove one, add another. The group is a label, not a container that locks anything.

Try it
  1. Tap Add to breakfast. The sheet leads with your Usuals, then Combos you've logged, then foods.
  2. Pick Oatmeal. The fruit is a pick-one: choose Blueberries, Banana or Strawberries right on the row.
  3. Tap any amount chip (1 tbsp · 16 g). It opens in place: step it, or switch to grams. No sheet.
  4. Tap Almond butter's amount. It was imported with a vague serving: tap Say what it is, enter 2 tbsp, and it gains a real tbsp serving without leaving Eaten. ＋ Serving on any food lets you weigh and name your own ("bowl, 60 g").
  5. Tap ⇄ swap on Almond butter. Peanut butter is at the top because you've swapped it in before; pick it. The amount carries over (½ serving of almond butter is 16 g, so 1 tbsp of peanut butter).
  6. Open the group's ⋯. It lists what changed, offers Update the usual, and, because you've now swapped that line 4 times, Make it a pick-one.

The pieces

  • Pick-one lines. A usual line can hold several foods; you choose one when you add it. It starts on whichever you picked last time. This removes the "one recipe per fruit" problem without making you type.
  • Swap. Every Always line of a usual has ⇄ swap once it's on a day. It opens a picker: foods that have replaced this line before come first, with a count, then search. A swapped line also offers "Back to Almond butter". The amount carries over when the new food has a serving with the same name, otherwise the grams do. Pick-one lines don't show swap; their options are on the row.
  • Swapping never changes the usual. It changes that day's entry, like removing one food and adding another in its place. The group gets a Changed chip, and its ⋯ offers Update the usual (the new food becomes the Always food) or Make it a pick-one (both foods become options). The second is suggested once you've swapped the same line 3 times.
  • Inline amounts. The chip shows serving and grams together. Tapping opens a stepper that moves by half a serving (or 5 g), with the food's named servings and grams as unit pills. The kcal on the right updates as you step. This is the one I'd build first on its own: it helps every entry, not only usuals.
  • Fix a serving where you notice it. The amount editor has ＋ Serving. For a food stuck on a vague serving, it asks what the label's serving is ("2 tbsp"), works out 1 tbsp from the grams it already knows, and renames the vague one "2 tbsp". For any food you can also name a portion and type its grams. Either way it saves to the food, so it's there next time, and the entry switches to it.
  • Long names and many options. A pick-one shows as buttons only when it has 3 options or fewer and every name fits (20 characters). Otherwise the row shows the chosen food and a Pick one · 2 of 5 button that opens a list: one full-width row per option, the name wrapping instead of cut off, with brand, amount and kcal. Names are never truncated: "Natural Creamy Peanut Butter" and "Natural Crunchy Peanut Butter" differ only in the middle. Each food can have an optional short name ("Crunchy PB"), set on the food in Library or right here from the usual. It's used in every usual and wherever space is tight, while search, Library and the meal's own rows keep the full name. Short enough names turn the list back into buttons. Try Add to after dinner → After dinner cereal, and the Yogurt bowl with five nut butters.
  • Combos you've logged. Foods you've eaten together in the same meal three or more times show up as one-tap suggestions with no setup. "Yogurt + granola" is a usual you never had to make.

Angle 3: save a usual from a meal

Forage already has this for recipes (#788: Select on a meal, tick items, Save as a recipe). The prototype keeps that flow and changes what it makes: a usual, with a switch per line for pick each time.

Try it
  1. On Lunch, tap ⋯, then Select. Tick the bread, turkey and cheese.
  2. Tap Save 3 as a usual. Turn on Pick each time for the cheese; it lists the other cheeses you've logged as its options.
  3. Save, then Add to dinner: your new usual is at the top.

Recipe or usual?

I'd keep both and draw the line here. A recipe is something you cook in a batch and eat a serving of: chili, banana bread. It logs as one entry, has steps, servings and walkthroughs, and goes on the plan and shopping list. A usual is an assembly you eat in one go: it logs as its parts, has no steps or servings, and is about speed in Eaten. Whether usuals also appear on the Plan (and shopping) is a later question; I'd leave it out of the first version.

Keeping a usual current

When a logged group differs from its usual, the group's ⋯ says how ("Peanut butter instead of Almond butter · Oats 50 g, was 40 g") and offers Update Oatmeal. Nothing updates on its own; the log is a record of what you ate, the usual is a starting point.

Managing usuals, and where combos come from

Usuals get a segment in Library, between Foods and Recipes. The same screen lists the combos Forage found in your log, so you can turn one into a usual or hide it. The Library tab works in the other angles too.

Try it
  1. Open Oatmeal. Remove Strawberries from the fruit, add Chia seeds with ＋ Option, step the oats, or reorder lines. Changes save as you go.
  2. Turn Pick each time off on the fruit: it becomes a fixed Blueberries line. Turn it back on.
  3. Go back. Under Combos you've logged, open the sandwich. It shows the meals it came from and typical amounts. Name it and save it: it moves up into your usuals and leaves the combo list.
  4. Open another combo and Hide it.

The Usuals list

  • Each row: name, foods in short (a pick-one shows as Blueberries / Banana), kcal as it starts, the meal it's usually for, and how often and how recently you've used it. Most recently used first.
  • ＋ New usual starts an empty one. Most usuals will come from a meal (angle 3) or a combo, though.
  • The editor has no Save button: each change saves on its own, like the rest of Forage (#785). Deleting asks once and leaves logged days alone.
  • "Usually for" ranks the usual in the Add sheet: Oatmeal comes first when you add to breakfast.

How combos are found

  1. Take the last 60 days of the log and group it into meals (a date and a slot), each a set of foods. Amounts are ignored here.
  2. From each meal, take every set of 2 to 5 of its foods.
  3. Count the meals each set appears in. Keep sets seen in 3 or more.
  4. Drop a set when a bigger one holds it in at least 80% of the same meals. "Bread + turkey + cheddar" hides "bread + cheddar".
  5. Join sets of the same size that differ by one food into one combo with a pick-one line.
  6. Drop combos a usual already covers (all their foods appear in it), and ones you've hidden.
  7. Typical amounts are each food's median across those meals.

The Add sheet shows the top 3, the meal you're adding to first, then by count, then by how recently you had it. Library shows all of them. Nothing is stored except the hidden list.

    What I'd skip

    • Turning recipes into usuals or the reverse. They answer different questions; a recipe logged "as ingredients" would need a serving maths that people don't think in.
    • Nested groups (a usual inside a usual).

    A possible order

    • Inline amount editing, with ＋ Serving: helps every entry, no schema change.
    • Copy a meal, push and pull: one action, FoodLog only.
    • Usuals: the tabs, logging as a group, pick-one lines, saving from a meal.
    • Combos, swap suggestions and Update usual, once there's grouped history to learn from.
